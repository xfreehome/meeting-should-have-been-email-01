## The joke

> The meeting that should have been an email ended with one decision: another meeting, to read the email out loud together.

One line, 121 characters. The setup names the most universal office complaint — a meeting that should have been an email — and the turn escalates it instead of resolving it: the meeting's only decision is to schedule *another* meeting. The punchline lands last, on the absurd image of a whole room gathered to read the email out loud together.
